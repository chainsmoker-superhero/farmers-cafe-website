Why We Exist
Every farmer deserves world-class intelligence—not just industrial corporations.

Modern agriculture is full of uncertainty. Weather is unpredictable, input costs fluctuate, and trustworthy advice is hard to find. Large industrial farms have access to expensive software, agronomists, and data teams. Independent farmers are often left to guess on their own.

We founded Farmers Cafe to change that. We believe great agricultural intelligence should be accessible to everyone who works the land. FarmerGPT puts an experienced, trusted advisor right in your pocket—simple to use, respectful of your knowledge, and always ready to help.
